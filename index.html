04 / Latent Vision

Latent Vision.

Generative models don't make our pictures. They haunt them.

Latent Vision is our research practice: a sandbox where we use diffusion models to scout moods, test compositions, and pre-visualize impossible sets — before a single shutter clicks.

Every final frame is still made by humans, with cameras, in real rooms. The machine is a sketchbook — not a shortcut.
